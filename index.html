My Quran – Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026 · App: My Quran (Android package com.kamrafi.myquran)

My Quran is a Quran reader with audio recitation, bookmarks, a Khatmah (completion) planner and a tasbih counter. It is free, has no account, no ads and no analytics. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Everything you create in the app stays on your phone.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following only in its private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Your last reading position, bookmarks and bookmark notes.
  • Your Khatmah plan and which pages you have read.
  • Your tasbih phrases, counts and daily routines.
  • Your settings (reading mode, theme, text size, reciter, reminders).
  • Recitation audio files you have played or downloaded, so they can play offline.

You can delete the downloaded audio in Settings. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all of it. Android may include this data in your own device backup if you have backup turned on; that backup is controlled by your Google account, not by us.

Internet use

The Quran text, page layout, translation and fonts are built into the app and work offline. The app uses the internet only to download recitation audio, one file per ayah, from everyayah.com, when you press play or download a surah. As with any website, that server receives your device's IP address and a standard request for the audio file. No identifier, account or personal information is added to these requests. everyayah.com is operated independently of this app.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
InternetTo download recitation audio.
NotificationsFor the Khatmah and dhikr reminders you turn on, and for the playback controls while audio plays.
Foreground service (media playback)So a recitation keeps playing when you switch apps or lock the screen.
Display over other appsOnly for the optional floating tasbih counter, so you can keep counting while you use other apps. It is asked for only when you start the floating counter, and you can turn it off at any time in Android settings.
Run at startupTo restore your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.
Vibrate, keep awakeTasbih tap feedback, and keeping audio playing reliably.

Sharing

If you choose "Share" on an ayah, the app opens Android's share sheet with the ayah text. What happens next is up to you and the app you pick.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
